Which generated creative styles actually earn their spend

Thirty days of paid social spend on AI generated ad creatives, broken down by generation style and channel. It separates styles that win the click from styles that win the sale, flags where fatigue is eating returns, and sizes what moving budget out of below floor styles would do to blended ROAS. The same cohort signals are what an ad ranking model would learn from when deciding which style to recommend to an advertiser.

NOTEIllustrative data modeled on Higgsfield AI's public product structure. Not a real customer's or the company's actual data. Built by Vishal Kumar as a candidate work sample, not an official Higgsfield AI artifact.
